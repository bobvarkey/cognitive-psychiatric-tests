## 📱 App Overview

**App Name:** Cognito  
**Tagline:** Clinical Mental Health Assessments  
**Subtitle:** Comprehensive Assessment Tool for Mental Health Professionals

### Description (Short - 30 words)
Evidence-based clinical assessment platform with 30+ validated scales for psychiatric, cognitive, and neurological evaluations. Secure, HIPAA-compliant, designed for mental health professionals.

### Description (Long - 300 words)
Cognito is a comprehensive clinical assessment platform that brings evidence-based psychological and psychiatric evaluation scales to your fingertips. Designed specifically for mental health professionals, clinicians, and healthcare providers, it provides instant access to over 30 clinically validated assessment tools.

**Key Features:**
- **30+ Assessment Scales** - Comprehensive tools covering:
  - Cognitive assessments (MMSE, FAB, Dementia screening)
  - Psychiatric evaluations (HAM-D, ADHD screening, Delusions)
  - Neurological assessments (Consciousness, Catatonia, Movement disorders)
  - Substance use evaluations (Drug interactions, Side effects)
  - Risk assessments (Fall risk, Psychopathy screening)
  - Specialized scales (Sleep, Pediatric, Endocrine)

- **Instant Scoring** - Automatic calculations and interpretation
- **Clinical Validation** - All scales evidence-based and widely recognized
- **Secure & Compliant** - HIPAA-compliant with enterprise-grade security
- **Fast & Intuitive** - Complete assessments in minutes
- **Organized by Category** - Easy navigation through assessment types

**Use Cases:**
- Initial psychiatric evaluations
- Treatment monitoring and progress tracking
- Diagnostic clarification
- Medication side effect monitoring
- Cognitive decline assessment
- Risk stratification

**Specifications:**
- Duration: Most assessments complete in 5-20 minutes
- Items: 500+ total clinical items across all scales
- Scoring: Instant, with interpretation guidelines
- Data: Secure local storage, no cloud transmission
- Compliance: HIPAA, clinical standards adherence

**Professional Tools for Professional Clinicians**

Not intended for self-diagnosis. All results should be interpreted by qualified mental health professionals in clinical context.

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

### Privacy Policy
The app does not collect, store, or transmit personal patient data. All assessment responses are:
- Stored locally on the device only
- Not synced to cloud services
- Not shared with third parties
- Subject to device-level encryption (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore)

### Data Handling
- No user accounts required
- No login system
- No analytics tracking
- No advertising
- No external API calls
- Completely offline-capable

### HIPAA Compliance
✅ No patient identifiers stored  
✅ No health information transmitted  
✅ Local data storage only  
✅ No business associate agreement needed

## 📝 Terms of Use (Key Points)

### Professional Use Only
This app is intended for licensed mental health professionals and should not be used for self-diagnosis. All results must be interpreted in clinical context by qualified professionals.

### Limitation of Liability
The app provides assessment tools but is not a substitute for professional clinical judgment. Users are responsible for all clinical decisions and patient care.

### No Patient Data Storage
This app does not store patient identifiable information and is not designed for patient record keeping. Clinicians are responsible for secure record management per HIPAA requirements.

### Offline Use
The app works completely offline and doesn't require internet for assessment administration.
